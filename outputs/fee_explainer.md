## Why the total charge on a trade can be more than brokerage

_For support: reusable explanation for users confused about charges beyond brokerage._

- Equity trades charge brokerage per executed order, the lower of ₹20 or 0.1% with a ₹5 minimum. Futures & Options charge ₹20 per executed order.
- When you sell shares, a DP charge applies per transaction, consisting of the depository fee and Groww’s fee.
- STT, stamp duty, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover charge and IPFT are regulatory/statutory charges levied by the exchange, not by Groww.
- An 18% GST is added on brokerage, DP charges, exchange transaction charges, IPFT and SEBI turnover charges.
- The overall trade cost includes brokerage plus DP, regulatory, statutory and GST charges, so it may exceed brokerage alone.
